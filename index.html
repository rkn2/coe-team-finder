CoE Team Finder

Paste a funding call and get three possible teams from Penn State College of Engineering faculty, each with reasons, the expertise still missing, and a draft intro email. It runs on your own Claude API key, and nothing is sent anywhere except to Anthropic.

1. Your Claude API key

Get one at console.anthropic.com. The key stays in this browser and only goes to Anthropic. A search costs roughly 10 to 30 cents with Opus and less with Sonnet.

2. The people

Loading the college roster...

Your CSV can add people or fix someone's research. It is matched on email and kept only in this browser. Use the columns from the download (name, email, department, title, research, keywords, recent_papers).
